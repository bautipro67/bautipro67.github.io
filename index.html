What every simulator has

  • Practice mode, Bad Time Simulator style: dodge the attacks in order, and if you lose your HP bar the attack restarts.
  • OPTIONS: volume, easy mode (half damage), hats for your SOUL and remappable controls.
  • Gamepad support (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch), touch controls on phones and a fullscreen button.
  • A Codes box under the game for secret codes.

Controls

Z or Enter: confirm   X or Shift: cancel   Arrow keys: move   Esc or P: pause. Click the page once to turn on the sound.
